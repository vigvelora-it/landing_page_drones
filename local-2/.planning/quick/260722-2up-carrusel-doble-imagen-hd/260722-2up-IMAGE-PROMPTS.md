# Prompts de generación — carrusel de tecnología

**Herramienta:** `image_gen` integrada de OpenAI
**Fecha:** 2026-07-22
**Formato común:** fotografía documental fotorrealista, horizontal 3:2, 1536×1024,
sin logotipos, texto ni marcas de agua.

## 1. Dron de mapeo en los Andes

> Create a photorealistic editorial photograph of a professional industrial mapping
> drone prepared for a precision land survey, on high-altitude Andean terrain in Peru.
> Show the complete drone from rotor tip to landing gear on a rugged equipment case,
> centered with breathing room, soft early-morning light, restrained blue-gray and earth
> palette. No people, logos, text, watermark, cropped propellers or sci-fi parts.

## 2. Posicionamiento GNSS RTK

> Create a photorealistic editorial photograph of professional GNSS RTK surveying
> equipment in a Peruvian highland construction corridor. Show a complete rover pole,
> receiver and controller in front plus a compact base station on tripod behind, with all
> primary equipment inside the frame, clean overcast daylight and technically credible
> geometry. No identifiable people, logos, text, watermark or impossible equipment.

## 3. Estación total e infraestructura

> Create a photorealistic editorial photograph of a robotic total station used for a
> precision topographic survey at a modern civil infrastructure project in Peru. Show the
> complete instrument and tripod with an unfinished bridge alignment behind, balanced
> horizontal framing and soft late-afternoon light. No identifiable people, logos, text,
> watermark, distorted tripod or duplicated instruments.

## 4. Fotogrametría aérea en minería

> Create a photorealistic editorial photograph of an aerial photogrammetry survey over an
> open-pit quarry in the Peruvian Andes. Show one complete professional mapping drone in
> controlled low flight and a small field operator from behind at a safe distance, with
> restrained morning light and geospatial documentary styling. No logos, text, watermark,
> military styling, cropped rotors or impossible drone anatomy.
